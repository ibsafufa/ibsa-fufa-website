IBSA FUFA — DEVICE CARE & IT SERVICES WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: website content
- styles.css: responsive design
- script.js: mobile menu and email contact form

BEFORE PUBLISHING:
1. Open index.html and replace YOUR_EMAIL@example.com in script.js with your real business email.
2. Replace the placeholder email text in index.html with the same email address.
3. Add your phone/WhatsApp details if you want visitors to contact you that way.
4. Test the contact form after replacing the email.

PUBLISHING:
You can upload these files to GitHub and deploy with GitHub Pages, or import the repository into Render.
For a custom domain, buy ibsafufa.com from a registrar and connect its DNS records to your hosting provider.
The domain's live availability and final price must be confirmed at checkout.
